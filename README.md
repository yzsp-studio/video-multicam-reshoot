## 🇨🇳 中文

把固定机位视频改编成 AI 多机位重拍，让同一段表演获得多个有明确拍摄时间和空间位置的视角。流程先分析原片与目标的实际差异，梳理事件、动作、道具状态与空间布局，提取镜头语言，再把侧面、后侧、俯拍、低位和物件视角安排到关键动作切点。通过分时段提示词驱动各机位素材，确认后按批次产出片段或重剪成片，默认使用生成音频，也支持只交付提示词不生成。全程保持身份、表演、空间连续性与动作时序。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 从原片梳理事件、动作、道具状态与空间布局
- 把侧面、后侧、俯拍、低位与物件视角安排到关键动作切点
- 为每个机位编写分时段提示词，而不是一条通用提示词打天下
- 确认后按批次生成片段，也支持只交付提示词不生成
- 保持身份、表演、空间连续性与动作时序
- 可接收目标案例，反推出想要的镜头设计方案

## 🇬🇧 English

Turns single-camera footage into an AI multicam reshoot, giving one performance several viewpoints with explicit times and camera positions. It analyzes the real gap between the source and the target, maps events, actions, prop states, and spatial layout, then extracts camera language and places side, rear, overhead, low, and object angles at motivated action beats. Timed per-viewpoint prompts drive each batch, and after your approval it generates clips or a finished recut, with native generated audio by default; prompt-only guidance is also supported. Identity, performance, spatial continuity, and action timing are preserved throughout.

### ✨ What it does

- Maps events, actions, prop states, and spatial layout from the source footage
- Places side, rear, overhead, low, and object viewpoints at motivated action beats
- Writes timed per-viewpoint prompts instead of one generic prompt
- Generates approved clips in batches, or stops at prompt-only guidance
- Preserves identity, performance, spatial continuity, and action timing
- Accepts optional target examples and reverse-engineers the shot design
